# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CheapCoin serves value-conscious consumers and crypto-native community members
who want one approachable application for connecting an EVM wallet, following
CHEAP, qualifying for tokenized-asset rewards, and discovering useful ways to
pay less. Groceries remain an important category, but the product can expand to
everyday goods, household costs, technology, travel, services, and verified
partner benefits.

## Product Purpose

Everything is expensive. CheapCoin went looking for cheap. The application
connects a speculative community token to visible, verifiable utility: a primary
CHEAP market, transparent creator-fee routing, strict COST Diamond Drops,
weighted-random CHEAP Surprise Drops,
deal discovery, partner benefits, education, and a public reward ledger.

Success means a user can quickly understand what they hold, which benefits they
qualify for, which RWA asset a drop uses, where that reward came from, and how to
verify every measurable claim onchain.

## Confirmed Network Direction

- Robinhood Chain is the only network for the new CheapCoin application.
- The Pump.fun Solana token is retired from future CheapCoin utility.

  Stock Tokens can be added as independently funded reward assets.

## Brand Personality

Resourceful, communal, transparent, and a little irreverent. The voice is
energetic and meme-aware without sounding like a casino or promising an outcome.
Finding something cheap should feel clever, participatory, and useful.

## Anti-references

Avoid pump-and-dump launch pages, fake countdowns, fabricated savings or
earnings, guaranteed-return language, dense DeFi terminals, nested card grids,
and interfaces that hide reward mechanics behind vague claims. Do not imitate a
brokerage or imply that CHEAP itself is equity.

## Design Principles

1. Show the loop clearly: trading produces CHEAP and COST fees, the treasury
   funds separate programs, and users can verify each named reward asset.
2. Lead with the next useful action for each connected user.
3. Prove every measurable claim with live data, methodology, and onchain records.
4. Treat RWA drops, partner perks, and deal discovery as one holder-benefits system.
5. Keep Web3 mechanics available for expert users while using familiar consumer
   language in the primary journey.
6. Label every reward asset by symbol, issuer name, canonical contract, source,
   and distribution transaction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for core flows. Never rely on green alone to communicate
state, maintain readable contrast and visible focus, support keyboard navigation
and reduced motion, and use plain-language wallet and transaction explanations.
